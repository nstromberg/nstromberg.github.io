I am thrilled to announce that two of our papers have been accepted to NeurIPS 2025! 
 - **[Thumb on the Scale](https://nstromberg.github.io/publication/2025-06-24-thumb)** elucidates the role of overparameterization during last layer retraining. We provide optimal weights which depend on the parameterization ratio $\delta=\frac{d}{n}$ and show that this weighting scheme leads to stronger generalization under distribution shift than standard weights.
 - **[CORAL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15933)** is a lightweight adaptation of standard diffusion architectures which utilizes a contrastive loss to discourage latent feature entanglement between classes. The discovery of this entanglement was quite surprising and the fix is quite elegant -- nice job [Esther](https://www.linkedin.com/in/esther-rodriguez-hernandez-903777374/)!

 I'm excited to see folks in person in San Diego! Please reach out if you are interested in either work!
